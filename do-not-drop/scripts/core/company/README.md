# company

Persistent state of the company mode (expansion, D-0220): CompanyState (D-0202) and every number in company_tuning.gd, CompanyNet (host facts and snapshots; CompanyLayoutNet checks and applies its shed layout requests and events, D-0910; CompanyTableNet the shared packing tables' box_* requests and events, D-0715, and CompanyPackages the DeliveryPackage each taped box becomes on every peer, D-0715.b; CompanyOrdersNet the day's order book on every peer, the host's plan and its order_added / order_expired events, D-0813; CompanyWalletNet the host's wallet_moved, money in or out that no request led to, set on every peer from the host's balance, D-0517 (CompanyNet.publish_wallet()); CompanyShopNet the shop's purchases, shop_buy / shop_bought, only the host's go through, D-0518; CompanySnapshot packs the snapshot and checks what it carries), CompanySave (D-0206), DayCycle (D-0203), CompanyCatalog (D-0216, every Resource of data/products, zones, vehicles, gates, suppliers and boxes by id; extra roots for tests, errors for repeated ids), GameClock (D-0219, adapter of modules/day_clock) and CompanyLobbyMeta (D-2009, what Steam shows of the company through modules/net_session's NetSteamPresence).
Nothing here draws or plays sound: UI and audio listen to EventBus (docs/arquitectura.md §10).
