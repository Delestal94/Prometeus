# business

Data and logic of the company mode ("Modo Empresa", expansion): ProductDefinition (D-0204), Inventory (D-0211, stock by product and location with reservations) OrderRequirement (D-0806, the six order requirements as data in `data/order_requirements/`) OrderBook (D-0210, active orders and their states, host authority) OrderRhythm (D-0803, how many orders a day and when they arrive) OrderGenerator (D-0801, the day's orders from a seed) OrderPricing (D-0502, quote and settlement of an order) PackedBox (D-0212, a box being packed: its cell grid, padding, tape, label and stamps) PackingStation (D-0701, a packing table: the open box and the products waiting beside it) OrderLabel (D-0707, the street and number a box label shows for its order) OrderPrinter (D-0805, the shed printer: one sheet per new order, the tray, who carries it and the table it lies on) OrderSheet (D-0714, what the order sheet on a packing table shows: the order against the box on the table) BoxToPackage (D-0213, a packed box turned into the DeliveryPackage the truck carries, with its trap) BuildMode (D-0901, warehouse build mode: state, raised camera and grid lines) WalkNavigation (D-0908, the walking grid employees will path over, rebaked with the layout) WarehouseStages (D-0905, the four shed sizes, their price and the upgrade; D-0906, the dock and exit areas no object can cover) DefaultShed (D-0911 and D-0620, the furnished shed a new company starts with, from `data/shed/default_shed.tres`: pieces placed through BuildPlacement, shelf stock, the first supplier pallets; CompanyShed in `scripts/gameplay/` draws it) DispatchZone (D-0716, the dispatch area: sealed boxes at rest inside it wait tied to their order until a vehicle is loaded) FixedCosts (D-0509, the day's rent) and what comes next.

Every number of the mode lives in `scripts/core/company/company_tuning.gd` (`CompanyTuning`, D-0202); the scripts here read it from there.
