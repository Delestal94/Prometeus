# data/shop/

Items of the company shop (`ShopItem`, D-0511); `CompanyShop.items()` reads every `.tres` here.
Vehicles (D-0512) are `vehicle_<fleet_key>.tres`, switched off (`enabled = false`) until their vehicle exists.
Machines (D-0513) are `<equipment key>.tres`; the ones whose machine does not exist yet (conveyor, packer) are switched off too.
Prices of the rest of the catalog: D-0514 (expansions);
numbers in `docs/expansion-distritos/diseno/economia.md` §4.
